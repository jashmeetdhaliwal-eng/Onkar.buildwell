02 — MATERIALS

Materials that form
the foundation.

Explore the role of each material in modern construction.

01
C

Cement

The primary binding material used to make concrete and mortar. It reacts with water to develop strength and bind aggregates together.

Binding • Concrete • Mortar
02
CS

Coarse Sand

Sand with relatively larger particles, commonly used in concrete and construction mixes to contribute to grading, workability and overall mix structure.

Concrete • Mortar • Grading
03
10

10 mm Aggregate

Small-size coarse aggregate used in concrete mixes. Its particle size helps achieve suitable grading and packing for particular applications.

Concrete • Grading • Packing
04
20

20 mm Aggregate

A common coarse aggregate size for structural concrete. It forms the load-bearing granular skeleton of the concrete mix.

RCC • Structural Concrete
05
FS

Fine Sand

Finer sand used where a smoother, more workable mix is required. It fills voids between larger particles and contributes to surface finish.

Mortar • Plaster • Finishing
06
FA

Fly Ash

A fine mineral material used as a supplementary cementitious material. It can improve workability and contribute to long-term concrete performance.

Blended Cement • Concrete
07
AD

Admixture

Chemical additives incorporated into concrete to modify properties such as workability, setting time, water demand or strength development.

Workability • Setting • Performance
08
CH

Construction Chemicals

Specialized products used for applications such as waterproofing, repair, bonding, protection and improving the durability of building components.

Repair • Waterproofing • Protection
09
RMC

RMC

Ready-Mix Concrete is proportioned and mixed under controlled conditions before delivery to site. It helps maintain consistent batching and efficient concrete placement.

Ready-Mix • Consistency • Site Efficiency
