02A — CONCRETE GRADE GUIDE

Choose the right grade.

A quick reference for common nominal concrete grades from M10 to M40. Final grade should be selected according to the structural design and project specification.

GradeCharacteristic compressive strength*Typical use / reference
M1010 N/mm²Blinding, leveling and non-structural applications
M1515 N/mm²General non-critical concrete work
M2020 N/mm²Common structural and residential applications
M2525 N/mm²Higher-strength structural applications
M3030 N/mm²Structural work requiring higher strength
M3535 N/mm²Higher-performance structural concrete
M4040 N/mm²High-strength structural applications

*Grade values refer to characteristic compressive strength at 28 days. Use the project engineer's approved mix/design specification for final selection.
