Our Materials

Construction Materials

A dependable range of essential materials and construction solutions for modern projects.

01

Cement

A fundamental binding material used for concrete, mortar and structural applications. Quality cement contributes to strength, durability and long-term performance.

02

Coarse Sand

Coarse sand is used in concrete and construction mixes to provide proper particle structure and support strength and workability.

03

10 mm Aggregate

10 mm aggregates are commonly used in concrete and reinforced concrete applications where controlled aggregate size is required.

04

20 mm Aggregate

20 mm aggregates are widely used in structural concrete, helping provide bulk, strength and stability within concrete mixes.

05

Fine Sand

Fine sand is used in mortar, plastering and selected concrete applications, helping achieve smooth texture and proper mix consistency.

06

Fly Ash

Fly ash can be incorporated into cementitious systems to improve workability and contribute to durable, resource-efficient concrete solutions.

07

Admixture

Concrete admixtures are added to modify specific properties such as workability, setting behaviour, water demand and performance.

08

Construction Chemicals

Construction chemicals support specialized building requirements including bonding, waterproofing, protection and concrete performance.

09

RMC

Ready-Mix Concrete is professionally proportioned and mixed concrete supplied for efficient, consistent and reliable construction applications.
