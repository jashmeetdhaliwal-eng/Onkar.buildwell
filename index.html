FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

How do I estimate the concrete quantity?

Multiply length × width × depth in metres to get cubic metres. Add an allowance based on your project conditions and confirm the final quantity with the site/technical team.

Which concrete grade should I choose?

The structural designer/project specification should determine the required grade. Use the grade guide here as a quick reference, not as a substitute for structural design.

Can I send my calculator result directly?

Yes. The calculator can create a pre-filled enquiry containing your dimensions, grade and estimated quantity.

What details should I include in an enquiry?

Include your name, phone number, project/site location, concrete grade, approximate quantity and preferred delivery date/time.
