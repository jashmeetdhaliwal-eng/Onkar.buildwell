Materials

Essential materials used in Ready Mix Concrete and construction applications.

↗

Cement

A primary binding material used to form concrete and provide strength and durability to construction.

↗

Coarse Sand

Used as a fine aggregate component to provide structure, workability and volume within concrete.

↗

10 mm Aggregate

Crushed aggregate used in concrete mixes where controlled particle size and good workability are required.

↗

20 mm Aggregate

Common coarse aggregate used to provide strength, stability and volume to concrete mixtures.

↗

Fine Sand

Fine aggregate that contributes to the overall grading, finish and workability of concrete.

↗

Fly Ash

A supplementary cementitious material used in selected concrete applications to improve mix performance.

↗

Admixture

Specialized ingredients added in controlled quantities to modify properties such as workability and setting.

↗

Construction Chemical

Specialized products used for construction, protection, repair and performance requirements.

↗

Ready Mix Concrete

Professionally proportioned concrete produced for consistent quality and delivered ready for placement.
