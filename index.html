Our Materials

Construction Materials

A range of essential construction materials and solutions for different project requirements.

01

Cement

A fundamental binding material used in concrete, mortar and structural construction. Quality cement helps provide strength, durability and long-term performance.

02

Coarse Sand

Coarse sand is commonly used in concrete and construction applications where proper grading and particle size contribute to strength and workability.

03

10 mm Aggregate

10 mm aggregates are used in concrete mixes and other applications where smaller-sized aggregate is required for suitable workability and finish.

04

20 mm Aggregate

20 mm coarse aggregate is widely used in structural concrete and helps provide the required strength, stability and load-bearing characteristics.

05

Fine Sand

Fine sand is used in mortar, plastering and selected concrete applications, contributing to smoothness, finish and workability.

06

Fly Ash

Fly ash can be used as a supplementary cementitious material in concrete. It can improve workability and contribute to durable and sustainable mixes.

07

Admixture

Concrete admixtures are added to modify properties such as workability, setting time, water demand and overall concrete performance.

08

Construction Chemicals

Construction chemicals support specialised requirements including waterproofing, bonding, repair, protection and concrete performance.
