Materials

The materials behind quality concrete.

A ready mix concrete plant combines carefully proportioned ingredients to produce concrete with the required strength, workability and durability.

01

Cement

The primary binding material that reacts with water and holds the concrete mixture together.

02

Fine Aggregate

Usually natural sand or manufactured sand used to fill spaces between coarse aggregate particles.

03

Coarse Aggregate

Crushed stone or gravel forming the main structural skeleton of concrete.

04

Water

Required for cement hydration and contributes to the workability of fresh concrete.

05

Admixtures

Specialized chemical additives used to modify concrete properties such as workability and setting time.

06

Fly Ash

A supplementary cementitious material used in suitable concrete mixes to improve selected performance properties.

07

GGBS

Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag, used as a supplementary cementitious material in appropriate mixes.

08

Mineral Additions

Materials such as silica fume or other approved additions may be used for specific concrete requirements.
