Construction Materials

Everything you need to build better.

A range of essential construction materials selected to support different stages of building and concrete work.

01 / MATERIAL

Cement

A fundamental binding material used in concrete, mortar and masonry. Cement provides strength, durability and structural stability when combined with the appropriate aggregates and water.

02 / MATERIAL

Coarse Sand

Coarse sand is commonly used in concrete and construction applications. Its particle size and texture contribute to the overall structure and workability of concrete mixes.

03 / AGGREGATE

10 mm Aggregate

10 mm aggregate is used in concrete and other construction applications where a smaller aggregate size is required for suitable grading and mix performance.

04 / AGGREGATE

20 mm Aggregate

20 mm aggregate is widely used in structural concrete. Properly graded aggregate contributes to concrete strength, stability and overall performance.

05 / MATERIAL

Fine Sand

Fine sand consists of smaller particles and is used in mortar, plastering and selected concrete applications. It helps improve finishing and mix consistency.

06 / MATERIAL

Fly Ash

Fly ash is a supplementary cementitious material used in concrete mixes. It can contribute to workability, durability and improved resource efficiency when properly proportioned.

07 / CHEMICAL

Admixture

Concrete admixtures are added in controlled quantities to modify properties such as workability, setting time, water demand and performance according to project requirements.

08 / CHEMICAL

Construction Chemicals

Construction chemicals support specialised building requirements such as waterproofing, bonding, repair, protection and improved concrete performance.

09 / CONCRETE

Ready Mix Concrete

Ready Mix Concrete is produced in controlled conditions and delivered to the construction site ready for placement. It helps streamline concrete operations and maintain mix consistency.

Ready Mix Concrete

Reliable concrete. Delivered for the job.

Ready Mix Concrete provides a practical solution for construction projects requiring consistent concrete mixes and organised site delivery.

With controlled batching and planned supply, RMC can help construction teams improve productivity and reduce the challenges associated with on-site concrete preparation.
