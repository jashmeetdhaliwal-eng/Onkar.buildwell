Our Materials

The Materials Behind
Strong Concrete.

A ready-mix concrete plant depends on carefully selected raw materials and supporting ingredients. Hover over each card to explore its role.

01

Cement

The primary binding material that reacts with water and holds the concrete mixture together.

02

Fine Aggregate

Sand used to fill voids between coarse aggregate particles and contribute to workable concrete.

03

Coarse Aggregate

Crushed stone or gravel forming the major solid skeleton of concrete.

04

Water

Required for cement hydration and for achieving the required workability of the mix.

05

Admixtures

Special ingredients used to modify concrete properties such as workability and setting.

06

Mineral Additions

Supplementary cementitious materials may be used where required for specific concrete performance.

07

Concrete Mix

Precisely proportioned combinations of ingredients prepared to meet project requirements.

08

Quality Control

Testing and controlled batching help maintain consistency throughout concrete production.
