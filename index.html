Our Products

Construction Materials

Reliable materials for strong, durable and efficient construction.

01

Cement

A fundamental binding material used in concrete, mortar and other construction applications. Quality cement contributes to strength, durability and structural stability.

02

Coarse Sand

Coarse sand is used as an important aggregate component in concrete and construction work. It helps provide structure, volume and stability to mixes.

03

10 mm Aggregate

10 mm aggregate is commonly used in concrete applications where controlled aggregate size is required for effective mixing and finishing.

04

20 mm Aggregate

20 mm aggregate is widely used in structural concrete and general construction, providing bulk, strength and stability to concrete mixes.

05

Fine Sand

Fine sand is used in mortar, plastering and finishing applications. Its finer particle size supports smoother and more workable mixes.

06

Fly Ash

Fly ash is a supplementary cementitious material that can improve concrete workability and contribute to more resource-efficient construction.

07

Admixture

Concrete admixtures are added to modify specific properties of concrete, helping improve workability, setting characteristics and performance when properly selected.

08

Construction Chemicals

Construction chemicals support specialised requirements such as waterproofing, bonding, protection, repair and enhanced durability of construction systems.
